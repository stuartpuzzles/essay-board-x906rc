Essay Listening Board

Private review queue — tap Play to have your phone read an essay aloud using its built-in voice. Keep the screen on and this tab in focus while playing (most mobile browsers pause speech when the screen locks). Move cards through the columns as you go, and leave feedback after listening.

Nothing syncs automatically. Feedback is saved only in this browser's local storage on this device — it does not update the files on your computer by itself. Use the button below (or the per-card "Copy Dispatch Prompt" button) to get your feedback back into the repo via Claude mobile.

Text-to-speech isn't available in this browser. Try Safari or Chrome on your phone.
